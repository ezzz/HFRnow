## Behaviour
- **Calendar** (`NoelCalendar`): Gregorian dates in the device's time zone (`TimeZone.autoupdatingCurrent`, read at each use, so a zone change during a run is followed), whatever its calendar (a Buddhist or Japanese year would name other keys); a season is named after the year of its December (the 2026 season ends on 02/01/2027). Phases: 01/12–24/12 `switchOn`, 25–26/12 `arrivalOnly`, 27/12–02/01 `packing`, otherwise `outside`. Selectable = the first two.
- **State** (device-only, ADR 0013 matrix E): `season.noel.<year>.arrivalShown`, `.arrivalPending`, `.optedOut`, `.previousThemeID`, `.packingShown`, `.themeID` (PR 4: which Noël theme is on, `noel-soft` or `noel-abuse`; absent or unknown reads as Noël soft, which every earlier build switched on); `season.noel.activeYear`, the season whose theme is on; toggles `season.automatic` and `season.animations` (both default on). A Noël theme of season Y is on exactly while `previousThemeID(Y)` is stored, and `activeYear` is Y then.
- **Scheduler** (`SeasonalThemeModel.sceneDidBecomeActive`, called on every scene activation): (1) a theme still on from any other season (`activeYear` ≠ Y: an earlier one, or a later one switched on under a clock that ran ahead and was set back) is restored silently; (2) in a selectable phase, the first activation sets `arrivalShown` and `arrivalPending`, switches Noël soft on when the phase is `switchOn`, « Thèmes de saison automatiques » is on and the season is not opted out, and the alert stays pending until answered, across launches (dropped when the window closes); (3) in `packing` with a theme on, the first activation sets `packingShown` and restores (`packed`, animated only when animations are allowed: the elves of PR 3), later ones restore silently; (4) `outside` with a theme on: silent restore. Each activation returns the events it caused (`arrival`, `packed`, `restoredSilently`).
- **Noël soft**: the current look, the user's appearance and dark style, with legacy's red accent drawn over it (`ThemeAppearance.applying`). Nothing of the "Thème" settings is written, so restoring is dropping `previousThemeID` and gives the user's theme and accent back exactly. The saved id is LOT-188's stored `theme_id` (`legacy` when none is stored; the first build always saved `legacy`, which reads the same). **Over a gallery theme** (operator decision (a), 2026-11-12): the red accent is drawn over Legacy only; a gallery theme keeps its own accent (its `accentText`, held to its contrast rules) and gets the snow and the crossings.
- **Noël abusé** (PR 4): a theme of the LOT-188 engine, `Themes/noel-abuse/noel-abuse.theme.json`, family « Noël » (so never in the gallery), single variant (« clair = sombre »), `fontDesign` rounded, decorations `noel-full`. Picked by hand in « Thèmes de saison » inside the window (never switched on automatically: the operator's default is Noël soft); moving between the two Noël themes keeps the theme saved when the first was switched on; packed by the elves and restored like Noël soft. `ThemeAppearance.applying(.noelAbuse, catalog:)` takes its tokens, font design and decorations over whatever the user's theme is (Legacy or a gallery one), without writing `theme_id`; its fir-green background imposes the dark appearance like any single-variant theme; a build without the file draws the user's theme. No falling snow and no crossings (Noël soft's). **Palette**: the draft tokens of `docs/assets/themes/tokens/themes.json` (cream `surface`, red text tint) fail LOT-188's rules once the dark appearance is imposed (native text is the system's white: 1.06:1 on cream; red `accentText` 1.43:1 on the green), so the file keeps the board's red and gold on a darker fir green (`#092E1C`, see the art below) and reads as a dark theme: `surface` a slightly lighter green (`#0B3A22`), cream text, gold `accentText` and `link`, red `surfaceRaised` and spoilers; the bundled-theme contrast test is green. Cream cards as on the board need native rows to read `surface`, which is #842.
- **Noël abusé's art** (`ThemeScreenBackdrop`, `NoelAbuseLayout`): behind the content, on the screen background of every navigation container and split column (`appScreenBackground()`, each column its own): a pattern of small stars (64 pt grid), the garland repeated along the top below the status bar with an ornament hanging from each piece, the tree (30 % of the height, at most 260 pt) in the bottom trailing corner and three gifts in the leading one, standing on the home indicator's inset (the keyboard never moves them); the tree is also capped so that it and the gifts share 90 % of the width (230 pt on a 402 pt iPhone), and neither is drawn when it would be under 40 pt (a column under about 70 pt wide, or a screen under about 134 pt high). Static (it stays with Reduce Motion), one layer faded to 8 %: even a white pixel there keeps the text native screens draw on the background ≥ 4.5:1 — the system label and the secondary label (60 % white: section headers and footers) of the dark appearance, and the tint (THEMES.md §4 rule 7, tested). That secondary label is why the fir green is darker than the board's (`#092E1C`; the board's `#0F4D2E` gives it 4.09:1 with no art at all). **Trade-off**: at 8 % the art is at most 1.27:1 against the background, below rule 7's 3:1 for a decoration on a dark background and fainter than §2's « lots of colour »; text contrast wins, and the operator judges it on a device (#893). Hidden from accessibility, no hit testing. **Rebuild**: Noël abusé over Legacy changes the screen fill's structure (`.system` → `.theme`), so switching it on or off rebuilds the screens it wraps, as any change between Legacy and a gallery theme does; once a year that change is not the user's own: its packing, when the elves are done on a clear screen. Elves cut by the keyboard, a sheet, an alert or the lock past their first second never drop the theme then: it stays drawn (`held`, decorations hidden) until that scene's screen is clear again, or the scene leaves, so no screen is rebuilt while input comes up. Kept, not engineered away: one structure for Legacy and themes would change Legacy's standard path, which LOT-122/188 keep pixel-identical to no modifier. With Noël abusé on, Réglages › Thème says its pickers apply once the season is over.
- **Arrival alert** (`SeasonalArrivalAlert`): « Thème de Noël activé » / « Thème disponible dans les réglages » [OK, cancel role] [Désactiver] (the title was « Oh Oh Oh ! » until #1174), a system alert (spoken by VoiceOver). Each scene asks the model every second while an arrival is pending; it is presented only when the app lock does not cover the screen, nothing is presented over the window's root (sheet, composer, cover, another alert) and the keyboard is down (THEMES.md §4 rule 5), and by one scene only (iPad with two windows: the other gets it if the first goes away unanswered). « Désactiver » restores and sets `optedOut`; either answer clears `arrivalPending`. PR 2 plays the sleigh before it when `sleigh` is true.
- **Snow**: 28 procedural flakes (`SnowField`, fixed seed) in a `Canvas` driven by `TimelineView(.animation(minimumInterval: 1/30, paused:))`. Drawn over the root content in `NoelBand`: a 104 pt top band (the navigation bar with a large title, about 96 pt) and a 64 pt bottom band inside the safe area, so no large title, navigation button, tab bar, status bar or home indicator is crossed; sheets, the keyboard and the lock cover are above it. One band for the whole window: on an iPad split view the columns share it (an inline-title column keeps about 50 pt without snow under its bar). No hit testing, hidden from accessibility. Flakes fall only with animations allowed (setting on, Reduce Motion off); the timeline is paused while the scene is not active, in Low Power Mode and while a list or the reader scrolls (`SeasonalScrollActivity`: `onScrollPhaseChange` on the Catégories, topic and MP lists, the web view's pan gesture and deceleration for the reader).
- **Piled snow** (static, so it stays with Reduce Motion): the operator's snow-pile render as two caps on the top edge of the Catégories card, 11 pt high, reaching 3 pt below the card's top edge (behind the card, which covers their base; less than a Catégories row's 8 pt top padding in any case) (`SnowPileLayout.capFrames`); they are the header of the card's section (`SeasonalSnowCapSection`, Noël soft only: otherwise the rows are given as they are), since a cell clips what leaves it, so the card covers their base; and two drifts, at most 16 pt high, in the bottom safe area, in the corners either side of a 160 pt clearance for the home indicator (`bottomFrames`); none on a window without a bottom safe area. Neither reaches a line of text (THEMES.md §4 rule 7).
- **Arrival sleigh**: when the arrival fires with animations allowed, `NoelCrossingModel.queueArrival()`; the alert waits (`alert(for:screen:)` returns nil) while the sleigh is due or crossing. The sleigh is the render of Santa's sleigh pulled by two reindeer harnessed in front, drawn facing the trailing edge; it crosses one scene's band from one side to the other, a quarter of the window height (at most 240 pt), in 3 s, tilted by at most 15°, mirrored as a whole when it goes right to left and turned along its line (`CrossingPath.facing`), with one light haptic. It waits for a clear screen like the alert, stops when the keyboard, a sheet, an alert or the lock comes up, and a tap closes it (THEMES.md §4 rule 3). Without animations it is dropped and the alert comes alone. It is in-memory: an app killed before it played shows the pending alert alone at the next launch.
- **Packing elves** (PR 3, `NoelPackingModel`, `NoelPackingLayer`): the first activation 27/12–02/01 with a Noël theme on restores the store at once (step 3 above: a kill meanwhile loses nothing) and, with animations allowed, queues the packing in memory: the theme it took down stays drawn (`SeasonalThemeModel.drawnTheme`: the red accent, the snow, the drifts and the caps) until the elves have played. They wait, like the alert, for a clear screen (no lock, sheet, alert or keyboard) in a scene that is active itself (each scene's layer reports its own phase, so on iPad another window going to the background never ends or blocks them), play in one scene only, for 4.5 s: up to three elves (the operator's `noel_elf` render, a fifth of the window high, at most 180 pt, never taller than the band) rise from the bottom of the band at even fractions of its width, hop while « Allez on remballe » shows in a capsule, and go back down, while the snow and the drifts fade out (`NoelPackingLayout`). A tap ends them (THEMES.md §4 rule 3); the keyboard, a sheet, an alert, the lock, or their scene leaving the foreground or going away end them at once, except in their first second, when they wait and play again, at most twice (an alert of the same launch, such as the seasonal icon's question, would otherwise take them away before they are seen). Ended by their scene leaving, the user's theme shows at once; ended by the keyboard, a sheet, an alert or the lock, the theme they packed stays drawn, decorations hidden (`held`), until that scene's screen is clear again (PR 4, for Noël abusé's fill: see below). The overlay is hidden from VoiceOver, which hears « Allez on remballe » as an announcement. Without animations nothing is queued: the user's theme shows at once. Elves still waiting when the packing days are over are dropped at the next activation (playing ones finish). No haptic: rule 4's haptic is for crossings.
- **Crossing objects** (`NoelCrossingModel`, injected clock and random source): foreground time runs while at least one scene is active (each scene's layer reports its own phase, `sceneChanged(_:active:)`; PR 3: before, one app-wide flag let another iPad window stop a crossing), background time never counts; a scene gets a crossing only while it is active itself, and a crossing stops when its own scene leaves; the gap is drawn uniformly in 20–90 min at launch and at each crossing, and restarts at each crossing, so two crossings are never less than 20 min of foreground time apart (a new launch starts from zero). Once spent, with Noël soft on, animations allowed and a clear screen, one scene gets one object, 64 pt high, for 2.2–3 s: the small sleigh (faces its travel), a star, a gift in three colours, an ornament or a snowflake (upright: no front). Its path (`CrossingPath.random`) enters by a random edge, fully outside, and leaves by the opposite one, fully outside, at a random tilt (at most 30°) inside the band, which clips it. One light haptic per crossing through `HapticFeedback` (the haptics setting applies; none without animations, so none with Reduce Motion). Each scene's layer asks every 15 s (every 0.5 s while the sleigh waits), never per frame, and draws a `TimelineView` only while its object crosses.
- **Settings**: Réglages › Thème shows « Thèmes de saison » only in a selectable phase: « Thème habituel » / « Noël soft », then the two toggles.

## Edge cases
- Two activations the same day, or a cold launch after a background return: the arrival and the packing play once per season (`arrivalShown`, `packingShown`).
- App not opened during the packing days: silent restore at the next activation, however many seasons later (step 1, `activeYear`).
- App killed before the arrival alert was answered (or the alert held back by a sheet or the keyboard): it shows again at the next launch while the window is open.
- 25–26/12 first launch: the alert shows, nothing is switched on; Noël soft can still be picked by hand until 26/12 and is packed on 27/12.
- « Thèmes de saison automatiques » off: the arrival alert still shows (as on 25/12), nothing is switched on. *(to confirm with the operator, see the PR)*
- Reduce Motion or « Animations de saison » off: the arrival is the alert alone, packing restores without the elves, no snow falls, nothing crosses and nothing buzzes; piled snow stays (static).
- A sheet or the keyboard comes up while an object crosses: it stops at once; the gap restarted when it began.
- A rotation, split view or Stage Manager resize during the arrival sleigh restarts the layer's watch: the sleigh is queued again (`interrupt`) and plays again before the alert, while less than 15 s (`arrivalReplayWindow`) have passed since it first started; after that the alert comes alone. The layer asks only once a size has held for 0.3 s, so the many sizes of one divider drag do not start and cut the sleigh at each step (#840 round 3). An ordinary object cut that way just ends.
- iPad with two windows: one crossing at a time, in one scene; a scene that goes away stops its crossing.
- The first frame of a launch outside the window never shows a theme left on (`refresh` ignores it before the restore).
